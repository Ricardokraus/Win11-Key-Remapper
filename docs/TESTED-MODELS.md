## Tried it on yours? Tell us! 🙌

Whether it worked or not, that's how this list grows and how problems on other
models get fixed. It takes two minutes:

1. Open the
   [**Works on my laptop** form](https://github.com/Ricardokraus/Win11-Key-Remapper-Acer-Nitrosense/issues/new?template=works_on_my_laptop.yml)
   (you need a free GitHub account).

2. Write your laptop model (it's on the sticker under the laptop) and your
   Windows version (<kbd>Win</kbd>+<kbd>R</kbd>, type `winver`).

3. Say whether it works.

4. Copy the codes of each special key you tried (the NitroSense key,
   <kbd>Fn</kbd>+<kbd>Win</kbd>…): in the settings window, *Keys* → click the key
   under *Your key*, press the key, and its codes appear under it.

5. Add anything odd you noticed and click **Create**.

The same form is in the app: settings window → *About* → *Tell us it works on
your laptop*.
